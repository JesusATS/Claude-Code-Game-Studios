## Game
Lux Aeterna — 2D pixel art turn-based RPG, PC (Steam), Episode 1 as complete chapter.

## Visual Direction
"Luminous Shadow" — warm light vs. dense darkness. Pixel art style referencing Sea of Stars and Octopath Traveler.

## One-Line Visual Rule (Wall Rule)
"Every frame must answer: where is the warmth, and what is it fighting against?"

## Three Visual Principles

### 1. Light Earns Its Real Estate
Warm light is never decorative — each source must have a diegetic reason.
Design test: choose layouts where light sources have named reasons over ambient warmth fills.
Pillar: Story Earns Its Emotion

### 2. Silhouette Carries the Feeling
Every character sprite must read as a distinct emotional state from silhouette alone at 64x64 or smaller.
Design test: choose clearer silhouette over richer internal detail when ambiguous.
Pillar: The Company Changes You

### 3. The World Is Older Than the Visit
Every explorable environment must contain at least one visual element predating the player's arrival.
Design test: choose worn/layered over clean when ambiguous — clean reads as constructed for player.
Pillar: The World Has Memory

## Guest Color Persistence Rule
Guest accent colors linger in UI after departure. This is not decoration — it is Principles 1 and 3 applied at UI layer. Governs status screen, ability list, party display.

## Color Language (Established)
- Warm amber/gold = safety
- Cold blue-white = threat
- Guest accent colors = identity persistence
